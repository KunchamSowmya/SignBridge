About SignBridge AI

An AI-powered two-way Indian Sign Language communication system designed to reduce communication barriers between sign-language users and non-sign-language users.

🤟

Sign → Speech

The webcam captures hand gestures. MediaPipe extracts hand landmarks, and a trained Random Forest model predicts the corresponding sign.

🎤

Speech → Sign

Spoken English is converted into text and matched with available sign-language videos.

🧠

Artificial Intelligence

The system uses computer vision, machine learning and speech technologies to provide real-time communication.

🌐

Accessibility

SignBridge AI aims to make communication easier between deaf or hard-of-hearing users and people unfamiliar with sign language.
